# Reviewing a shot

Select a shot in the shot list, or open a saved session and select one of its
shots. The target displays the saved trace window, its phase boundaries and hold
zone when those data are available.

The controls below the target reset to the beginning, play or pause, and seek
through the shot window. The time label shows progress within that window.
Replay controls are disabled when no usable replay is loaded. The mouse wheel
and zoom controls adjust the target view.

Use **Space** to play or pause an enabled replay and **Up** or **Down** to
select adjacent shots when focus is outside an input or dialog. See
[shortcuts](shortcuts.md).

The left pane shows all session shot positions on a compact target above a
scrollable shot list with scores and offsets. Select a numbered marker or list
row to highlight the shot and load its replay. Shots without positions remain
in the list. The overview centres on the median horizontal and vertical shot
positions and automatically zooms to the group, with padding for the shot
markers. Tight groups show the relevant inner rings rather than the whole
face. All positioned shots, including outliers, remain visible during replay.
Adding or removing a shot updates the framing.

The app logo sits at the top of the navigation rail. The header contains the
pane controls. Use the left and right pane icons to collapse either side.
Click an icon again to restore its pane. Hover over an icon for its **Hide shots**,
**Show shots**, **Hide session** or **Show session** tooltip. The camera preview
remains available through the camera control in the navigation rail.

At narrower window widths, the panes sit below the target to preserve its
working area. Each pane can still be collapsed independently. Pane transitions
respect the system reduced-motion setting.

The **Zoom** slider, minus and plus buttons change the main target scale
around its centre. Drag right or scroll up to enlarge it. The percentage is
relative to the fitted target face. Choose **100%** in the percentage dropdown
to fit the face, or **Auto** to centre on the median shot position and fit all
positioned shots, like the session overview. Auto updates as shots change.
Using the slider, wheel or zoom buttons returns to manual zoom. The zoom menu
supports arrow-key navigation, Enter to select and Escape to dismiss.
Wheel and button zoom use the same limits. Zoom leaves the session overview
unchanged.

The **Replay** toolbar shows labelled play/pause and reset controls beside the
shot timeline. Select a shot with a saved trace to enable playback.
